Q1. WHAT PROBLEM DOES YOUR PROJECT SOLVE?
================================================================================

--- LONG ---

Someone loses money to a UPI scam at 9:14 at night. By 9:16 they know. A
beneficiary bank can still place a hold on the account the money went to - but
only while the money is still sitting in it, before it is split across mule
accounts and moved onward.

They are sent to cybercrime.gov.in, which asks them to pick a category, register,
and wait for an OTP - often on the same phone the scammer may still be calling -
and then to complete the full police complaint before it will accept anything.

Two jobs are merged into one form. A bank needs a handful of facts within minutes.
An investigation needs everything else over weeks. The portal paces both at the
speed of the slower one, so the urgent half is trapped behind the slow one. It is
an information-architecture problem, not a technology one: the form was designed
as police intake and then asked to also be an emergency stop button, and nobody
re-sequenced it when that second job arrived.

Golden Hour splits the report in two and sends the urgent half first - a nine-field
freeze packet with no login, no OTP and no required field, because a freeze
request should never be blocked for being incomplete. The statement is collected
afterwards, unhurried, against the same acknowledgement number.

The sequence is not my invention. The 1930 helpline already works this way: a short
list of facts, a system-generated acknowledgement number, then the full complaint
on the portal within 24 hours. The website simply never adopted it.

--- SHORT ---

Someone loses money to a UPI scam at 9:14 at night; by 9:16 they know. A bank can
still freeze the account the money went to, but only while it is still sitting
there. Instead they are sent to cybercrime.gov.in to pick a category, register,
and wait for an OTP - often on the same phone the scammer may still be calling -
and to finish the whole police complaint before anything is accepted. Two jobs
with different deadlines are merged into one form, paced at the slower one. Golden
Hour sends the bank's half first: nine fields, no login, no required field. The
1930 helpline already works this way; the website does not.

Q2. WHAT SPECIFIC FEATURE DID YOU BUILD OR IMPROVE FOR ROUND 2?
================================================================================

--- LONG ---

The guarantee that the model may not guess - and this round, the work that turned
it from a claim into something you can watch and check.

The rule: every field the model reads is re-validated on the server, and anything
whose shape is wrong for the field it claims to be is marked UNREADABLE rather
than sent, however confident the model was. The stakes are asymmetric, which is
the whole reason. A missing transaction ID means a bank works with what it has. A
wrong one means it freezes the wrong account while the real one empties.

Round 2 is where that stopped being an assertion. Three things were built:

An extraction evaluation, which did not exist before. 75 fields across generated
screenshots and text: 74 correct, and 40 of 40 read from screenshots with nothing
invented - the worst of three passes, not the best. The screenshots are generated
by a script that writes the answer key from the same object that draws the image,
so a case cannot disagree with its own ground truth. Fields whose expected answer
is UNREADABLE are cut out of the image entirely, so a value returned for one is an
invention with no argument available.

A demo case you can watch it happen in. Every blank in the product used to come
from the model declining to read a field, which demonstrates the prompt behaving
well rather than the guarantee holding. One case now returns an eleven-digit
reference at 93% confidence where a real one is twelve digits. The server refuses
it on shape, and the screen shows you the value it threw away. A test fails if
that case ever stops being refused.

And the failure, published. A dictated "fastcart dot pay at samplebank" came back
as a different but perfectly well-formed account. Shape checking cannot catch that
and never will. It is on /honesty, next to everything else this does not do yet -
along with what changed since Round 1, on /changes, and the measured timings with
their sample size, on /evidence. All three are reachable from the live link
without an account.

--- SHORT ---

Server-side validation - the model may not guess. Every field is re-checked for
shape and marked UNREADABLE rather than sent wrong, because a missing transaction
ID is survivable and a wrong one freezes the wrong account. Round 2 built the
proof: an extraction eval that did not exist before (75 fields, 74 correct, 40 of
40 from screenshots with nothing invented - the worst of three passes, not the
best), and a demo case where you can watch the server refuse an eleven-digit
reference the model was 93% sure of. One value still escaped a shape check, and
that is published on /honesty rather than left out.

NOTES BEFORE YOU PASTE
================================================================================

1. Q2 deliberately matches minute two of the video. A judge who reads the form and
   then watches should hear the same answer twice. Do not swap in a different
   feature here.

1b. Q1 does not say the first hour "decides whether the money comes back", and an
   earlier draft of this file did. That is a magnitude claim, and /evidence
   explicitly refuses to draw one: the MHA was asked in Parliament for recovery
   against losses and the answer does not contain the word "recovered". What is
   sourced is the mechanism - a bank can hold the account while the money is still
   in it. Claiming more in the form than the site claims about itself is the one
   inconsistency that would cost you everything else.

2. "Worst of three passes, not the best" is worth leaving in. Most people quote
   their best run; saying which one you quoted is the cheapest credibility in the
   whole form.

3. Do not turn "nine fields" into "the nine facts a bank requires". Nine is this
   project's count of its own freeze fields. What a bank actually requires is not
   a number anyone here has counted, and /evidence refuses to guess it.

4. The 1930 claim is sourced to the MHA/I4C instruction sheet for reporting
   financial cyber fraud, which is marked "For Delhi Only". "The helpline already
   works this way" is what that document supports. Do not upgrade it to a national
   mandate.

5. If this is a team entry, switch "I" to "we" in the 250-word summary in
   SUBMISSION.md as well, and include your teammate's registered email.
